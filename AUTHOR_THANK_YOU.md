# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to James Cuin, Davide Carbone, Yanbo Tang, and O. Deniz Akyildiz
for developing and sharing the SOSMC perspective on optimization with
intractable gradients. The paper makes the interaction between particle
reuse, effective sample size, and stochastic optimization easy to study.

This repository is an independent educational evidence audit, not the
authors' implementation or an endorsement by the authors. The conditional
certificates and the explicit MNIST negative are labeled narrowly so that
bounded observations are not mistaken for general theorem or experiment
verification.
